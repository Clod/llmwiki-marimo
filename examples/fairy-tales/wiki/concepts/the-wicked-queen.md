# The Wicked Queen

## Definition
The Wicked Queen is a character from the fairy tale "Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs," known for being Snow White's stepmother who is driven by vanity and jealousy to eliminate Snow White, ultimately leading to her own downfall (Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf).

## Key Characteristics
- Stepmother to Snow White (Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf).
- Motivated by vanity and jealousy (Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf).
- Seeks to eliminate Snow White (Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf).
- Her actions lead to her own downfall (Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf).

## Context
The Wicked Queen is a central antagonist in the fairy tale "Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs," representing themes of vanity, jealousy, and the consequences of malicious intent. Her character is pivotal in driving the narrative forward, as her actions set the stage for Snow White's trials and eventual triumph.
