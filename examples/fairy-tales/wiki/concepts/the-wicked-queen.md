# The Wicked Queen

## Definition
The Wicked Queen is the stepmother of Snow White, known for her extreme vanity and jealousy, which lead her to make several attempts on Snow White's life (Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf).

## Key Characteristics
- Stepmother to Snow White (Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf)
- Driven by vanity and jealousy (Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf)
- Makes multiple attempts to kill Snow White (Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf)

## Context
The Wicked Queen is a central antagonist in the fairy tale "Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs." Her character embodies the themes of envy and the dangers of vanity, which are common motifs in fairy tales. Her actions propel the narrative forward, highlighting the contrast between her malicious intent and Snow White's innocence.
