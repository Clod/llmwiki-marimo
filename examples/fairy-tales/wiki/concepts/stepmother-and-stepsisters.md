# Stepmother and Stepsisters

## Definition
The stepmother and stepsisters are characters in the Cinderella story who serve as antagonistic figures. They subject Cinderella to a life of servitude, embodying themes of jealousy and familial conflict (Cinderella.pdf).

## Key Characteristics
- Antagonistic figures in the story (Cinderella.pdf)
- Subject Cinderella to a life of servitude (Cinderella.pdf)
- Represent themes of jealousy (Cinderella.pdf)
- Involved in familial conflict (Cinderella.pdf)

## Context
In the broader context of fairy tales, the stepmother and stepsisters are archetypal characters that often embody the challenges and obstacles faced by the protagonist. Their role in "Cinderella" highlights the common narrative theme of overcoming adversity and the triumph of virtue over malice.
