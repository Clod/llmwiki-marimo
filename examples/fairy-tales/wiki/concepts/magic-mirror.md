# Magic Mirror

## Definition
The Magic Mirror is an enchanted object that the Queen uses to ascertain who is the fairest in the land, ultimately revealing Snow White's superior beauty (Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf).

## Key Characteristics
- Enchanted object with the ability to speak.
- Provides truthful answers about beauty and fairness.
- Central to the Queen's quest for validation of her beauty.

## Context
The Magic Mirror plays a crucial role in the story of "Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs," as it is the catalyst for the Queen's jealousy and subsequent actions against Snow White. This object is a classic example of enchanted items in fairy tales that serve as a plot device to reveal truths and drive the narrative forward.
