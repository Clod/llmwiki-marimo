# The Seven Dwarfs

## Definition
The Seven Dwarfs are a group of miners who provide refuge and protection to Snow White after she escapes from the Queen's assassination attempt (Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf).

## Key Characteristics
- Consist of seven individual dwarfs, each with distinct personalities.
- Work as miners, extracting valuable resources from the earth.
- Live together in a cottage in the forest.
- Offer shelter and safety to Snow White, becoming her protectors (Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf).

## Context
The Seven Dwarfs play a crucial role in the fairy tale "Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs," serving as both a source of comic relief and as pivotal characters who aid Snow White in her time of need. Their actions contribute significantly to the narrative, highlighting themes of friendship, loyalty, and the triumph of good over evil (Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf).
