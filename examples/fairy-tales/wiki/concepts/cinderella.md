# Cinderella

## Definition
Cinderella is the central character of the Cinderella story, a heroine who is characterized by her goodness and patience under abuse. Her transformation in the story depends on her kindness rather than on her birth.

## Key Characteristics
- Shows goodness and patience while suffering abuse.
- Is contrasted with her stepsisters, whose behavior is marked by vanity.
- Her transformation is tied to her kindness, not to her social origin or birth.

## Context
Within the story, Cinderella serves as a point of contrast with her stepsisters. The narrative sets her virtues against their vanity, which makes her moral qualities the basis for her change in fortune. This emphasis places character and conduct above birth as the source of her transformation.
