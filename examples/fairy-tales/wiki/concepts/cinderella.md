# Cinderella

## Definition
Cinderella is the protagonist of a classic fairy tale, renowned for her kindness, beauty, and resilience in the face of adversity, particularly the mistreatment she endures from her stepfamily (Cinderella.pdf).

## Key Characteristics
- Known for her kindness and compassion (Cinderella.pdf).
- Possesses notable beauty, which is often highlighted in the tale (Cinderella.pdf).
- Demonstrates resilience and perseverance despite being mistreated by her stepfamily (Cinderella.pdf).

## Context
Cinderella is a central figure in fairy tale literature, often symbolizing the triumph of good over evil and the virtues of patience and humility. Her story has been retold in numerous cultures and adapted into various forms of media, underscoring its universal appeal and moral lessons.
