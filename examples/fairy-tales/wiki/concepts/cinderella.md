# Cinderella

## Definition
Cinderella is a fictional character renowned for her kindness and resilience, who overcomes adversity with the help of magical assistance to achieve happiness and forgiveness (Cinderella.pdf).

## Key Characteristics
- Known for her kindness and gentle nature (Cinderella.pdf).
- Displays resilience in the face of adversity (Cinderella.pdf).
- Receives magical assistance, often depicted as coming from a fairy godmother (Cinderella.pdf).
- Achieves happiness and forgiveness, typically culminating in a transformative event such as a royal ball (Cinderella.pdf).

## Context
Cinderella is a central figure in a classic fairy tale that has been adapted into numerous cultural narratives and media. Her story is often used to illustrate themes of virtue rewarded, the triumph of good over evil, and the transformative power of kindness and forgiveness. The tale of Cinderella has been retold in various forms across different cultures, each adding unique elements while retaining the core narrative of overcoming hardship through inner strength and external magical aid (Cinderella.pdf).
