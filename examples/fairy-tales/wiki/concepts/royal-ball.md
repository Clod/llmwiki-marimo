# Royal Ball

## Definition
A royal ball is a grand and formal event typically hosted by royalty, characterized by its opulence and exclusivity. In the context of fairy tales, it often serves as a pivotal plot device where significant character interactions and transformations occur.

## Key Characteristics
- **Opulence**: The event is marked by luxurious decorations, attire, and settings.
- **Exclusivity**: Attendance is usually limited to nobility or those with special invitations.
- **Transformation**: Often a setting for character transformations, such as Cinderella's magical change in appearance (Cinderella.pdf).
- **Romantic Encounters**: A common setting for romantic developments, as seen with Cinderella and the prince (Cinderella.pdf).

## Context
In literature and storytelling, a royal ball often acts as a catalyst for the plot, bringing together characters from different social standings and facilitating key interactions. It is a setting where societal norms can be temporarily suspended, allowing for unexpected developments and resolutions, such as the prince's admiration for Cinderella's beauty and grace, which sets the stage for the story's resolution (Cinderella.pdf).
