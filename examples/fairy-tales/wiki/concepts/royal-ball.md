# Royal Ball

## Definition
A royal ball is a grand and formal dance event typically hosted by royalty, serving as a significant social gathering. In the story of Cinderella, the royal ball is a pivotal event where Cinderella meets the prince, marking a transformative moment in her life (Cinderella.pdf).

## Key Characteristics
- Hosted by royalty, often in a palace or grand venue.
- Features formal attire, such as gowns and suits.
- Includes dancing, music, and often a banquet.
- Serves as a social event for networking and forming alliances.
- In fairy tales, often functions as a plot device for character transformation or romantic encounters (Cinderella.pdf).

## Context
In literature and storytelling, the royal ball often symbolizes opportunity and change. It provides a setting where characters from different social standings can interact, leading to significant plot developments. In "Cinderella," the royal ball is crucial as it allows Cinderella to transcend her oppressed life and connect with the prince, ultimately altering her destiny (Cinderella.pdf).
