# Little Red Riding Hood

## Definition
Little Red Riding Hood is a central character in a classic fairy tale, symbolizing innocence and naivety, which ultimately leads her into danger (Little Red Riding Hood.pdf).

## Key Characteristics
- Represents innocence and naivety (Little Red Riding Hood.pdf).
- Central character in a classic fairy tale (Little Red Riding Hood.pdf).
- Her journey and interactions often serve as a moral lesson about the dangers of trusting strangers (Little Red Riding Hood.pdf).

## Context
Little Red Riding Hood is a well-known character in the realm of fairy tales, often used to convey moral lessons about caution and the potential perils of naivety. The story has been adapted and retold in various cultures, highlighting its universal themes and enduring relevance in literature and popular culture.
