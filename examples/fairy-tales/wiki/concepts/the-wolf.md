# The Wolf

## Definition
The Wolf is a cunning predator featured in the story of "Little Red Riding Hood," known for deceiving Little Red Riding Hood and her grandmother, ultimately consuming both (Little Red Riding Hood.pdf).

## Key Characteristics
- Cunning and deceptive nature
- Predator instincts
- Ability to disguise intentions
- Consumes both Little Red Riding Hood and her grandmother (Little Red Riding Hood.pdf)

## Context
The Wolf is a central antagonist in the fairy tale "Little Red Riding Hood," representing the dangers of naivety and the threat posed by deceptive individuals. The character serves as a cautionary figure, illustrating the importance of vigilance and skepticism in the face of potential threats.
