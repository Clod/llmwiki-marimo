# The Wolf

## Definition
The Wolf is a cunning antagonist in the story "Little Red Riding Hood," symbolizing deception and predatory danger. It ultimately leads to the demise of both the grandmother and Little Red Riding Hood (Little Red Riding Hood.pdf).

## Key Characteristics
- Represents deception and cunning behavior (Little Red Riding Hood.pdf)
- Acts as a predatory threat to the protagonists (Little Red Riding Hood.pdf)
- Responsible for the demise of the grandmother and Little Red Riding Hood (Little Red Riding Hood.pdf)

## Context
The Wolf is a central figure in the tale of "Little Red Riding Hood," embodying the dangers that can lurk in seemingly safe environments. It serves as a narrative device to illustrate the themes of caution and the consequences of naivety, often found in folklore and fairy tales.
