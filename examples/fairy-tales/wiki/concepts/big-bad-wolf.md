# Big Bad Wolf

## Definition
The Big Bad Wolf is the wolf antagonist of the tale *Little Red Riding Hood*, who pursues and deceives the girl and her grandmother in order to devour them.

## Key Characteristics
- Races the girl to the grandmother's house by the shorter path.
- Imitates the girl's voice to gain entry to the house.
- Later imitates the grandmother's voice to gain entry.
- Devours both the girl and the grandmother.

## Context
The Big Bad Wolf is the central threat in *Little Red Riding Hood*. Its actions depend on deception: by mimicking the voices of the two characters, it overcomes the protection of the grandmother's house. The figure is an entity within the tale's narrative rather than a separate concept, and its role is defined by the events of the story.
