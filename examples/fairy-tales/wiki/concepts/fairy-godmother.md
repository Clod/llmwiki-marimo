# Fairy Godmother

## Definition
The Fairy Godmother is a magical figure in the story of Cinderella who assists the protagonist by using magic to transform everyday objects into a grand carriage and attire, enabling her to attend the royal ball (Cinderella.pdf).

## Key Characteristics
- Possesses magical abilities (Cinderella.pdf).
- Transforms mundane items into extraordinary ones, such as a pumpkin into a carriage (Cinderella.pdf).
- Acts as a benevolent guide and helper to Cinderella (Cinderella.pdf).

## Context
The Fairy Godmother is a central figure in fairy tales, particularly in "Cinderella," where she represents the intervention of magic and fate in the protagonist's life. Her role underscores themes of transformation and the possibility of change, often serving as a catalyst for the narrative's progression towards a happy ending (Cinderella.pdf).
