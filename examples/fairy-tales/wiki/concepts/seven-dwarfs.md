# Seven Dwarfs

## Definition
The Seven Dwarfs are fictional characters from the fairy tale "Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs," who are miners living in the forest. They provide refuge and protection to Snow White after she escapes the Queen's assassination attempt (Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf).

## Key Characteristics
- **Occupation**: Miners (Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf)
- **Role**: Protectors and caretakers of Snow White (Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf)
- **Number**: Seven distinct individuals
- **Personality Traits**: Each dwarf is characterized by unique traits, often reflected in their names, such as Grumpy, Happy, Sleepy, etc.

## Context
The Seven Dwarfs play a crucial role in the narrative of "Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs," serving as both a source of comic relief and a pivotal support system for Snow White. Their presence highlights themes of friendship, loyalty, and the contrast between innocence and malice in the broader fairy tale genre.
