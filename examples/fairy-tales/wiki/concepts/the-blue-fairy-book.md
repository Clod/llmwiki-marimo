# The Blue Fairy Book

## Definition
The Blue Fairy Book, edited by Andrew Lang, is a collection of fairy tales that includes 'Little Red Riding Hood'. It was first published in 1889 and is now in the public domain (Little Red Riding Hood.pdf).

## Key Characteristics
- Edited by Andrew Lang
- First published in 1889
- Contains a variety of fairy tales, including 'Little Red Riding Hood'
- Public domain status

## Context
The Blue Fairy Book is part of a series of fairy tale books edited by Andrew Lang, known as the "Lang's Fairy Books". This series is significant in the literary domain for its role in popularizing fairy tales and making them accessible to English-speaking audiences. The inclusion of well-known stories like 'Little Red Riding Hood' highlights its importance in preserving and disseminating traditional narratives.
