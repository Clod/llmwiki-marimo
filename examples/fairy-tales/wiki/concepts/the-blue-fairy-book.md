# The Blue Fairy Book

## Definition
The Blue Fairy Book, edited by Andrew Lang, is a collection of fairy tales that includes well-known stories such as Little Red Riding Hood. It has played a significant role in the popularization of these tales in Western culture (Little Red Riding Hood.pdf).

## Key Characteristics
- Edited by Andrew Lang
- Contains a variety of traditional fairy tales
- Includes the story of Little Red Riding Hood
- Contributed to the popularization of fairy tales in Western culture

## Context
The Blue Fairy Book is part of a series of fairy tale collections by Andrew Lang, known as the "Lang's Fairy Books." These collections have been instrumental in preserving and disseminating traditional fairy tales, making them accessible to a broad audience and influencing the perception and adaptation of these stories in Western literature and media.
