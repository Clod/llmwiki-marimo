## Summary

Cinderella, a kind and gentle girl mistreated by her stepmother and stepsisters, is transformed by her fairy godmother to attend a royal ball. She captivates the prince but must flee before midnight, leaving behind a glass slipper. The prince uses the slipper to find and marry Cinderella, who forgives her family and lives happily ever after.
