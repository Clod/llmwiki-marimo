## Summary

Little Red Riding Hood is a fairy tale about a young girl who is sent by her mother to visit her ill grandmother, carrying custard and butter. On her way, she encounters a wolf who tricks her into revealing her destination. The wolf reaches the grandmother's house first, eats her, and then deceives Little Red Riding Hood, ultimately eating her as well.
