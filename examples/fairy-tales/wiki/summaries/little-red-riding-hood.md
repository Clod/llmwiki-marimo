## Summary

Little Red Riding Hood is a fairy tale about a young girl who is sent by her mother to visit her sick grandmother, bringing custard and butter. On her way, she encounters a wolf who tricks her and reaches the grandmother's house first, ultimately eating both the grandmother and Little Red Riding Hood. The story highlights themes of innocence, deception, and danger in the guise of familiar figures.
