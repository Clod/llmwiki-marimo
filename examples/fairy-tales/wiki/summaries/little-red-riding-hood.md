## Summary

A retelling of the classic fairy tale in which a young girl in a red hood travels through a forest to visit her sick grandmother and meets a wolf who disguises his voice, eats the grandmother, and then the girl. The text is a public domain version from The Blue Fairy Book (Andrew Lang, 1889), with dialogue that sets up the wolf's deception and its fatal outcome.
