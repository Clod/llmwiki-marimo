# Snow White And The Seven Dwarfs

**Source:** Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.pdf | **Type:** pdf | **Pages:** 6 | **Ingested:** 2026-11-17

## Summary

A retelling of the fairy tale Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs, in four parts. The jealous Queen, consulting her magic mirror, orders the huntsman to kill the fairest princess, Snow White, who finds refuge with seven dwarfs. After the Queen's poisoned apple kills her, a prince's kiss revives her, and the wicked Queen dies at the wedding.
